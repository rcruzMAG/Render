# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Professional creatives and small studios — designers, architects/visualization
artists, and video/VFX people — who run AI image and video generation locally on
their own GPU. They are visually literate and have high taste; they judge a tool
partly by how it looks and feels. They want the power of ComfyUI-class models
(FLUX.1, Wan 2.2, SDXL, ControlNet, LoRAs) without the node-graph friction, and
they move fluidly between drafting and finishing. Their context is focused
creative work, often in long sessions, frequently on large/color-accurate
displays.

## Product Purpose

Render Studio is a local web app that turns a powerful-but-intimidating
generation engine (ComfyUI) into a calm, polished studio. It exists to make
running the best open-weight models feel premium and effortless: one-click
setup, opinionated presets per task, and a clean workspace where the generated
media — not the chrome — is the hero. Success is when a pro creative trusts it
for real client-facing work and chooses it over both the raw node graph and
generic cloud tools, because it feels like a professional instrument they own.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and confident. Three words: **quiet, crafted, capable.** The
voice is plain and expert — it never shouts, never gamifies, never pads with
jargon or hype. Visually it should feel like a refined professional instrument:
restrained, spacious, and exacting, with every detail considered. Emotionally it
should evoke focus and trust — the steadiness of a tool that respects the user's
craft and gets out of the way of it.

## Anti-references

- **Toy-like / over-gamified UI** — no cartoonish styling, emoji-as-icons,
  confetti, bouncy gimmicks, or playful mascots. This is a pro instrument, not a
  consumer toy.
- **The raw ComfyUI node graph** — Render Studio is the calm layer *over* that
  complexity; it must never feel like wires, nodes, and exposed plumbing.
- **Generic admin/Bootstrap dashboard kits** — nothing templated or
  off-the-shelf; the craft should be visibly intentional.

## Design Principles

1. **The work is the hero.** Generated media gets the space, contrast, and
   focus; UI chrome stays quiet and recedes around it.
2. **Calm by default, depth on demand.** Lead with opinionated presets and a
   clean surface; reveal full parameter control progressively, never as an
   all-at-once settings dump.
3. **Precision over decoration.** Restraint, exact spacing, and typographic
   discipline (the Linear standard) communicate quality more than ornament.
4. **Quiet confidence.** Premium calm in the Apple/Final Cut spirit — generous
   negative space, content-forward, no hype.
5. **Respect the craftsperson.** Expert, plain language and predictable,
   trustworthy behavior; never patronizing, never gimmicky.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA.** Body text and meaningful UI meet 4.5:1 contrast (3:1 for
large text and essential non-text elements). Full keyboard navigation with clear,
visible focus states. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for all transitions and
progress animation. Do not rely on color alone to convey state (engine up/down,
job status) — pair with text or shape. Account for color-accurate / wide-gamut
displays common among the pro-creative audience.
